What happens onchain
  1. We publish a strategy: the assets and their target weights.
  2. The client signs once to create their own smart account and grant a limited trading permission.
  3. Prices move and the portfolio drifts from target.
  4. On schedule, or on demand through the API, the platform's agent swaps overweight holdings into underweight ones inside the client's account. Trades under $5 are skipped, and slippage is capped.
  5. The portfolio is back on target. The client keeps custody throughout and can withdraw at any time.
