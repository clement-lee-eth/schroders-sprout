Getting started

01How do I get started, and how long does it take?

About two minutes. Sign up with your email (we send a one-time code, so there's no password or seed phrase) or connect a wallet you already use. Answer five questions, pick what you're saving for, and approve your plan once.

02What is the minimum investment?

You can start from $5. Even small amounts hold every company in the portfolio at Schroders' weights, because each holding can be split into tiny fractions.

03Who is eligible to invest?

SPROUT is in pilot in selected markets. In some, including the UK, EEA, Singapore and Hong Kong, it's open to professional or qualified investors only. We confirm eligibility when we verify your ID.

04Can I use an existing wallet?

Yes. Connect the wallet you use today, or we'll create one for you at sign-up. Either way, it's your wallet and only you can take money out.

Your investments

01Who holds my investments?

You do. Your investments sit in your own account, not pooled in a fund with anyone else's. Neither Schroders nor Glider holds them, and only you can withdraw. Each holding is backed one-for-one by the real share, held with a regulated custodian; see Important information below for how this differs from owning a share directly.

02What are the fees?

During the pilot you pay a 0.30% trading fee on what's bought, taken automatically inside your portfolio. There's no account fee and no exit fee. Any ongoing management fee will be shown clearly before you invest.

03How do I withdraw?

Anytime, from the app. Sell to cash in your wallet, or move your holdings as they are to another wallet or provider without selling. Selling happens while US markets are open; moving holdings works around the clock.

04What are the risks?

Like any investment, your portfolio can fall as well as rise, and you may get back less than you put in. It isn't a bank deposit. There's also the risk that a provider in the chain, such as the share's issuer, runs into trouble, which is why every holding is backed by the real share with a regulated custodian.

05What happens if SPROUT or Glider ceases to operate?

Your investments stay in your own wallet either way. You could keep them, sell them, or move them to another provider. Nothing depends on SPROUT or Glider being there for you to own what you own.

Our investment approach

01What is Schroders' role?

A Schroders investment team chooses what you own, at what weights, and changes it when its view changes. You see every decision in the app, with the reason. Schroders never holds your money and can't withdraw it.

02How does rebalancing work?

Every day, Glider checks your portfolio against Schroders' targets and trades it back on track inside your own account. You approved this once, with one signature, and the permission can only rebalance, never withdraw.

03What happens when markets fall?

Nothing for you to do. Daily rebalancing quietly tops up what has fallen and trims what has risen, and your cash buffer softens the swings. You'll see what changed, and why, in the app.

04How is my portfolio tailored to me?

It starts from Schroders' model, then follows your choices: your risk level, an optional theme, and any companies you'd rather leave out. When Schroders updates its model, your personal mix follows at the next daily run.

Planning for the long term

01Can I save for more than one goal?

Yes. Each goal, such as retirement or a home deposit, gets its own portfolio and its own level, chosen for when you need the money.

02How does SPROUT get me ready for retirement?

A retirement goal runs on autopilot. As your date gets closer it steps down to steadier levels on its own, so a late fall in markets can't derail it. You see your progress as a monthly income, and when you retire you can turn it into a monthly paycheck or keep it invested.

03What happens if I change jobs or providers?

Your portfolio comes with you. Because you hold the investments yourself, you can move them as they are, without selling and without paperwork. No cashing out, and no forgotten pots left behind.

For Schroders

01Where do the tokenised shares come from?

From the issuers, Ondo Global Markets or Coinbase. Each token is backed 1:1 by the underlying US share, held with a custodian under the issuer's terms. Glider buys tokens for the portfolio and, for Ondo, sells by redeeming them with the issuer, which needs an open US market session. Tokens can move between wallets at any time. Broker, custody and dividend arrangements follow each issuer's terms and are being confirmed with compliance.

02Which tokenised shares does it use?

Either issuer. Ondo offers over 140 US shares and ETFs on Glider today; Coinbase offers 8 US mega-caps on Base. Each strategy runs on one chain, so Schroders can publish an Ondo edition, a Coinbase edition, or both, and add issuers as their shelves grow.

03How do partners distribute it?

Any wallet, exchange or neobank that integrates Glider's API can offer Schroders models to its own users. Schroders reaches new investors without building a new fund for each market.

04What are the fees, and who earns them?

Glider takes a trading fee automatically inside each portfolio (0.30% of traded volume in Bitwise's ATPs) and keeps its share; the rest goes to the distributing app under Glider's API terms. A model fee for Schroders, like Bitwise's 0.15%, needs a licensing agreement with Glider. Network fees are covered by Glider.

05Can Schroders see every investor across partners?

Not through the standard API. Each partner sees its own investors. Schroders would see total reach through reporting agreed with Glider, which protects investor data at the partner.
